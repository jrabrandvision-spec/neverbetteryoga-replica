About

Never Better Yoga is a unique online practice that empowers both the spiritual and the physical. This powerful combination of movement and message challenges and refreshes not only the body, but the mind and spirit. Never Better yoga is on a mission to help you progress on your personal journey to find your best-self.

The "Never Better" Community is served through live and ON-Demand classes delivered on the Never Better Ondemand Video Library, YouTube channel, facebook & Instagram. With 20 years of yoga and fitness experience, the practice is led by Susan Smelt, a Division I gymnast from University of Arizona. She applies her own personal journey to help empower others to grow, heal, and progress.
